India is renowned internationally for its rich fruit farming areas and wide range of quality fresh produce. The diversity and relative consistency of its climates from temperate to tropical and sub-tropical enables India to provide a reliable supply of a wide range of citrus, deciduous, stone fruit, sub-tropical and exotic fruits throughout the year.

Strategically located in Mumbai (Bombay), one of the largest Fruits & Vegetables marketing centres of India.

Our Products has over 20 years' experience and success in international trading, procuring and exporting of perishable food products.
