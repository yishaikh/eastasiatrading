WHO WE ARE

Experience you can trust. Produce you can be proud of.

East Asia Trading is a reputed importer and exporter of fresh fruits and vegetables from India, with more than two decades of experience.

Our understanding of seasonal and regional climatic variations helps us select quality export varieties at the right time for international markets.

We work closely with individual farms and agricultural and market specialists to advise on picking times, quality expectations and packing requirements for our clients' countries.

Customer satisfaction, consistency and reliability remain central to our approach. We continually seek improvements through in-house research and development.
